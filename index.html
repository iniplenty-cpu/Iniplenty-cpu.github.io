About Inibhunu Thomas

Inibhunu Thomas is a Nigerian cryptocurrency trader whose journey reflects discipline, continuous learning, resilience and faith. From his upbringing in Irrua, Edo State, to his academic background in Statistics and his career in cryptocurrency trading, his story is one of determination and personal growth.

Biography

I am Inibhunu Thomas, born on 2nd April 2000, the sixth child of the late Mr. Thomas Odanlumen. I was born and raised in Irrua, in a modest but value-driven family where discipline, perseverance, and faith were deeply rooted in our daily lives.

Growing up in such an environment taught me early on the importance of hard work, resilience, and the determination to rise above limitations.

My educational journey began at Foundation Nursery and Primary School, where I developed my early academic foundation, and I later attended St. Patrick’s College, where my curiosity and desire to succeed became more pronounced.

I went on to further my education at Auchi Polytechnic, where I studied Statistics. This field sharpened my analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and attention to detail, skills that would later play a significant role in shaping my professional path.

After my academic journey, I sought opportunities beyond conventional paths and found my interest in the world of cryptocurrency trading. What started as curiosity quickly turned into a passion.

The journey was not without its challenges, as the crypto space is highly volatile and requires discipline, patience, and continuous learning. However, through consistency, self-education, and practical experience, I was able to grow and establish myself as a certified trader.

Today, I stand as someone who has not only embraced the opportunities of the digital financial world but continues to evolve within it. I am committed to constant improvement, always learning new strategies, adapting to market trends, and striving to reach greater heights in my trading career.
